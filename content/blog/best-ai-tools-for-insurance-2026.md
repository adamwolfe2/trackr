## 2. Shift Technology — Claims Fraud Detection

Shift Technology is the leading AI fraud detection platform in insurance, deployed at carriers covering billions in premiums across property, casualty, health, and life lines. Its models identify fraudulent claims and networks of fraudulent behavior with detection rates that far exceed rules-based systems.

The ROI is direct: Shift Technology claims customers recover $5-10 in fraud per $1 spent on the platform. For carriers with large claims volumes, the payback period is typically measured in months. Its integrations with claims management systems (Guidewire, Duck Creek) make production deployment practical.

## 3. Tractable — AI Damage Assessment

Tractable uses computer vision to assess vehicle and property damage from photos, automating the initial estimate in auto and property claims. Adjusters submit photos; Tractable's AI returns a detailed damage assessment and repair estimate in seconds.

The claims efficiency impact is significant: claims that require an adjuster visit or extended manual review can be handled with a photo submission and AI review. For high-volume personal lines carriers, Tractable's automated assessment reduces both cycle time and adjustment costs.

## 4. Gradient AI — Underwriting Intelligence

Gradient AI provides AI-powered underwriting analysis for commercial P&C and workers' compensation, with models that predict loss ratios more accurately than traditional actuarial approaches for specific risk classes.

The platform ingests policy data, claims history, and third-party risk signals to generate risk scores that help underwriters price risks more accurately — writing better business at better prices. For MGAs and carriers looking to improve their combined ratios on specific lines, Gradient AI's loss ratio prediction is the entry point.

## 5. Cape Analytics — Property Risk Intelligence

Cape Analytics uses satellite imagery and computer vision to assess property risk automatically — roof condition, property features, maintenance quality, and surrounding hazard factors — without requiring a physical inspection.

For personal and commercial property underwriters, Cape Analytics automates the property assessment that previously required costly inspections or incomplete data. It enables faster policy issuance while improving risk selection. The accuracy improvements for wildfire, hail, and water damage risk assessment are particularly strong.

## 6. Lemonade AI — End-to-End Insurance Automation (Model)

Lemonade isn't a vendor you buy; it's a model you study. Their AI-first insurance architecture — instant quoting, AI underwriting, automated claims resolution, and AI claims fraud detection — represents what's possible when insurance is built from the ground up for AI rather than adapted from legacy systems.

For insurtechs and carrier innovation teams, understanding Lemonade's architecture is more valuable than any vendor pitch. The takeaway: AI delivers its maximum value in insurance when it's embedded in core workflows, not bolted onto existing processes.

## 7. Majesco Underwriting AI — Policy Administration Intelligence

Majesco's underwriting AI works within their policy administration platform and via API integration with other core systems. It automates data ingestion from submissions, flags risk anomalies, suggests coverage recommendations, and generates underwriting memos.

For carriers on legacy policy administration systems looking to add AI capabilities without replacing their core platform, Majesco's API-first approach is a practical path. The integration library covers most major legacy PAS platforms.

## 8. Hi Marley — AI-Powered Claims Communication

Claims communication — keeping policyholders informed and gathering documentation — is a labor-intensive process that significantly affects NPS scores and litigation rates. Hi Marley is a conversational AI platform for claims communication, enabling two-way SMS and web-based communication with policyholders at scale.

AI-assisted message drafting helps adjusters respond faster. Automated status updates keep policyholders informed without adjuster involvement. For carriers with large claims volumes, Hi Marley meaningfully reduces inbound call volume and improves customer satisfaction.

## 9. Akur8 — AI Actuarial Pricing

Akur8 is an AI pricing platform for P&C insurance that uses ML to build more accurate rate models than traditional GLM approaches, with interpretability features that satisfy actuarial and regulatory review requirements.

The balance between predictive power and interpretability is the key differentiator: insurers need models that regulators can approve, not just models that perform well on holdout data. Akur8 is specifically designed to produce models that meet both requirements. Carriers using Akur8 report improved loss ratios and faster rate filing timelines.

## 10. Applied Epic with AI Features — Broker Management

For insurance brokers, Applied Epic remains the dominant agency management system. Its AI features in 2026 include automated data entry from carrier submissions, renewal activity triggers, coverage gap identification across client portfolios, and AI-assisted proposal generation.

For brokers managing hundreds of clients across multiple coverage lines, Applied Epic's AI reduces the administrative burden of account management and improves the consistency of coverage reviews. The ROI is in time recovered and error reduction rather than top-line growth.
