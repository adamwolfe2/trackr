## Spend Management Has Become a Strategic Function

Three years ago, the corporate card market was dominated by legacy issuers offering minimal intelligence. Today, Ramp, Brex, and Airbase have raised the floor dramatically — every modern finance and ops team expects real-time spend visibility, SaaS subscription detection, and automated approval workflows from their spend management platform.

The question isn't whether to modernize — it's which platform fits your company's stage, workflow, and spending patterns.

---

## Ramp — Best for Automated Cost Reduction

Ramp is built around a simple thesis: help companies spend less. Every product decision — AI-powered duplicate detection, subscription cancellation recommendations, vendor negotiation intelligence — serves that goal.

**What makes Ramp distinctive:**
- **Proactive saving suggestions**: Ramp actively surfaces spending anomalies, unused subscriptions, and vendor consolidation opportunities. Most customers report finding immediate savings in the first 30 days
- **SaaS subscription visibility**: Automatic detection of software subscriptions from card transactions, with utilization signals from SSO integrations (Okta, Google Workspace)
- **AI-powered vendor intelligence**: Ramp benchmarks your vendor pricing against similar companies and flags overpayment
- **AP automation**: Ramp+ adds accounts payable automation for non-card spend

**Pricing:** Core corporate card is free. Ramp Plus (AP automation, advanced controls): $15/user/month.

**Best for:** Companies that want a spend intelligence layer focused on cost reduction. Particularly strong for SaaS-heavy companies where subscription sprawl is the primary problem.

---

## Brex — Best for High-Growth Startups

Brex started as a corporate card for startups that couldn't qualify for traditional business credit. It has evolved into a broader spend management platform with an emphasis on international capabilities and VC-backed startup workflows.

**What makes Brex distinctive:**
- **No personal guarantee required**: Critical for startups where founders don't want to personally guarantee business credit
- **Higher credit limits**: Underwriting model is VC-funding-based, enabling higher limits than traditional issuers
- **Global capabilities**: Multi-currency cards and international expense management better than Ramp
- **AI expense management**: AI categorization and policy enforcement for expense reports
- **Brex Empower**: Extended spend management platform with budgeting, employee reimbursements, and AP

**Pricing:** Starter: Free (limited features). Premium: $12/user/month. Enterprise: Custom.

**Best for:** VC-backed startups that need high credit limits without personal guarantees, companies with international operations, and teams where international expense management is a core need.

---

## Airbase (now Paylocity Spend Management) — Best for Mid-Market Finance Automation

Airbase was acquired by Paylocity in 2023 and is positioned as a comprehensive spend management platform for mid-market companies — specifically those graduating from basic card solutions who need full AP automation, purchase orders, and multi-entity support.

**What makes Airbase distinctive:**
- **Full spend management stack**: Corporate cards + reimbursements + AP automation + purchase orders in one platform
- **Multi-entity support**: Better for companies with subsidiaries or multi-entity accounting structures
- **Deep accounting integration**: NetSuite, Sage Intacct, and QuickBooks integration with GL-level coding
- **Approval workflows**: Configurable approval chains for purchase requests and invoices

**Pricing:** Custom — Airbase targets mid-market and enterprise. Typically $500–$2,000+/month depending on user count and features.

**Best for:** Series B+ companies or mid-market organizations that have outgrown basic card solutions and need a full procure-to-pay workflow.

## How to Choose

**Choose Ramp if:** You're primarily focused on cost reduction and spend visibility. Strong SaaS subscription intelligence. Free core product makes the ROI case easy.

**Choose Brex if:** You're a VC-backed startup that needs high credit limits without personal guarantees, or you have significant international spending that needs multi-currency card support.

**Choose Airbase if:** You're a mid-market company that needs a full procure-to-pay solution — purchase orders, multi-entity support, deep accounting integration — not just a smarter card.
